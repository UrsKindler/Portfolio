### Patient-Derived iPSC Skeletal-Muscle Organoids in Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy

**Institution:** Ruhr University Bochum  
**Role:** Project design, scientific coordination, experimental execution, and computational analysis  
**Period:** 2019–2023  
**Publication:** [Cells (2025)](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4409/14/13/1033)

#### Objective

Investigate human satellite stem-cell function and myogenic-progenitor maturation in Duchenne muscular dystrophy using patient-derived iPSC skeletal-muscle organoids.

#### Approach

- Applied a 3D skeletal-muscle-organoid differentiation and long-term culture system.
- Planned and conducted scRNA-seq experiments using three DMD patient-derived iPSC lines and one healthy control.
- Developed R/Seurat workflows for cell-level quality control, filtering, normalization, highly variable gene selection, clustering, differential gene-expression analysis, UMAP, and pseudotime analysis.
- Controlled technical factors including sequencing depth, cell-cycle effects, and stress-associated genes.
- Integrated internally generated data with external human muscle-development reference datasets.
- Combined computational analyses with FACS-based cell selection and in vivo transplantation experiments.

#### Key Findings

- Reduced skeletal-muscle-progenitor populations in DMD organoids.
- Altered developmental trajectories and delayed maturation of myogenic progenitors.
- Increased activation of fibro-adipogenic progenitor populations in DMD samples.

#### Skills Demonstrated

`scRNA-seq` · `R` · `Seurat` · `Quality control` · `Differential expression` · `UMAP` · `Pseudotime` · `Reference data integration` · `hiPSCs` · `Organoids` · `FACS` · `Disease modelling`

#### Publication

(2025). *Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy Patient iPSCs-Derived Skeletal Muscle Organoids Exhibit a Developmental Delay in Myogenic Progenitor Maturation.* **Cells, 14**(13), 1033.
